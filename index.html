Who We Are

Built From the
Ground Up,
Across Africa.

We are a Pan-African trade facilitation firm headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya — founded and led by operators with direct, boots-on-ground experience moving commodities across the continent. Not consultants. Not intermediaries from afar. Trade practitioners who have done the work.

Our roots trace to 2014, when our founder established Wafalme Logistics and Transport Solutions — one of Kenya's early logistics technology ventures, recognised by USAID in 2017 as a top solution for post-harvest supply chain challenges in Tanzania.

Since then, our team has physically managed copper cathode movements from the DRC's Kolwezi mines to Dar es Salaam Port, executed gold verification mandates across Sierra Leone, Kenya and Uganda, and expanded trade corridors across East and Central Africa.

We bring that lived operational knowledge — combined with structured financial documentation, compliance rigour, and a wide network of regional relationships — to every mandate we take on.
